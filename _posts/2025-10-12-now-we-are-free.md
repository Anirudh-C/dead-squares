The well-informed reader knows that much has been said about *Maximus Decimus Meridius*, or *The Romantic*; the **Serpent-Gladiator** (for the uninformed reader, I provide the following [reference](https://anirudh-c.github.io/dead-squares/on-the-reckless-capture-of-pawns.html)). However, not much has been revealed on the nature of the relationship between him and I. Although, I would not consider him a bosom-friend, I have been in sporadic correspondence with him on various matters and on occasion have been blessed by his presence[^1]. I consider myself to be on first-name basis with dear old *Maximus*, while he treats me with an indifference akin to that of a housecat idly swatting a fly on a dreary summer afternoon. What follows are the contents of a postcard I received a long, long time ago from the very same, detailing an extraordinary sequence of events in Biel, Switzerland that the venerable *Maximus* was fortunate to have witnessed first-hand (and participated in, by his own admission). I do preface the ensuing with a warning to the reader regarding the braggadocious tone of *Maximus*. Although the events definitely did occur (to the knowledgeable reader, it is clear that *Maximus* writes about the awe-inspiring [victory](https://www.chessgames.com/perl/chessgame?gid=2201998) of GM Pia Cramling over GM Viktor Korchnoi), dear *Maximus* has the tendency to overplay his contribution. Nonetheless, I am ever grateful to him for writing to me regarding an occurrence so grand. Finally, I have left my annotations on *Maximus*' postcard in <span style="color:red">red text</span> which I believe may be of some aid to the reader.

# the postcard
July 22, 1984

To whomsoever it may concern,

As I lie in the infirmary, dazed and confused, I think of the good times, bad times. I wonder how many more times I must perish against the black mountain side struggling with the communication breakdown. <span style="color:red">(I think Maximus has discovered Led Zepellin)</span>. Alas, I must recall fondly the magnificent events that led to my maiming and request that these details be shared with the world.

[^1]: I have played the Vienna gambit featuring *Maximus* (albeit not in his true form) with ~~middling success~~ overwhelming frustration.
